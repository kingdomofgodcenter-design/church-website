Welcome Home

This world can feel lonely. Here, you don't walk the journey alone.

Kingdom of God Centre is a family of worshippers in Ottawa. We gather to grow in our relationship with God and with one another through Bible teaching, heartfelt worship, prayer and real fellowship.

Whatever your story, there is a place for you here. We can't wait to meet you.

Jesus at the centre
